# Positioning Framework & Statement · Roamly Groups

## 1. Positioning framework

- **For (target audience):** For community leaders, travel organizers, and trip coordinators who regularly plan multi-person travel experiences and are responsible for keeping everyone informed, aligned, and organized.
- **Who want / need:** Who need a simpler way to coordinate group travel without juggling multiple tools, chasing responses, managing spreadsheets, and constantly answering the same questions from travelers.
- **We offer:** Roamly Groups is a group travel coordination platform that helps organizers plan, manage, and communicate every aspect of a trip from a single place.
- **Because of (value proposition):** Because Roamly Groups centralizes every part of group coordination, organizers spend less time managing logistics, reduce planning chaos, and create smoother travel experiences for everyone involved.
- **Despite (barriers):** Despite asking organizers and travelers to adopt another platform when many groups already use familiar tools like WhatsApp, Google Sheets, Facebook Groups, email, and Venmo.
- **Better than (differentiation):** Better than stitching together multiple communication and planning tools, Roamly Groups provides a purpose-built group travel operating system that reduces organizer workload through a single, coordinated workflow.

## 2. Positioning statement

### First draft
Roamly Groups is a group travel coordination platform that helps organizers plan, manage, and communicate every aspect of a trip in one place. By replacing spreadsheets, messaging apps, emails, and payment tools with a single workflow, it reduces planning stress, saves time, and makes group travel easier for everyone involved.

### Pressure test
- **Test I ran:** A · Audience recognition
- **What it revealed:** The test revealed that the positioning statement is clear, but not yet distinctive or customer-centric enough. It explains what Roamly Groups does, but it doesn't immediately make the target customer feel understood.
The test showed that the positioning is functionally clear but emotionally weak.
It explains what Roamly Groups is, but it doesn't fully capture why organizers would switch from the tools they already use. The next iteration should focus more on the organizer's coordination burden and less on generic platform capabilities.

### Final statement
Roamly Groups helps community leaders, retreat hosts, and trip organizers coordinate group travel, payments, itineraries, and traveler communication from one place instead of juggling spreadsheets, group chats, and email threads. By eliminating the constant back-and-forth of chasing responses, answering questions, and keeping everyone aligned, it saves organizers hours of coordination work and makes group travel smoother for everyone involved.
