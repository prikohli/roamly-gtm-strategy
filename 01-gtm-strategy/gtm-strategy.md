## 2. GTM motion

- **Chosen motion:** Hybrid
- **Why:** Hybrid (Primary: PLG, Secondary: CLG)
Primary Layer: PLG (Product-Led Growth)
Users discover Roamly, create a group trip, invite friends, split payments, and book experiences directly in the product without assistance.
Secondary Layer: CLG (Community-Led Growth)
Each group organizer brings additional travelers onto the platform. Friends invite friends, creating organic growth through travel communities, social sharing, and referrals.

## 3. Primary goal

Acquire

## 4. The assumption I'm pushing back on

I’m pushing back on the recommendation to change the primary goal from Acquire to Expand.
While it’s true that Roamly Groups solves an existing customer pain point, the feature’s biggest strategic advantage is its ability to bring new users into the ecosystem. Every group trip naturally requires inviting friends who may not already be Roamly users. Unlike many expansion features that increase spend within a fixed user base, Roamly Groups creates built-in acquisition loops through invitations, shared itineraries, and split payments.
The evidence shows current users are already attempting group bookings, but the feature's value extends beyond fixing conversion leakage. Each successful organizer can introduce multiple new travelers to Roamly, creating a network effect that aligns directly with our PLG + CLG motion.
Therefore, I would keep Acquire as the primary goal while tracking Expand as a secondary outcome. Success is not only converting existing users who are struggling today, but also turning every group organizer into a channel for bringing new travelers onto the platform.

## 5. V1 GTM strategy one-pager

Product
Roamly Groups enables travelers to coordinate group bookings, split payments, and plan shared itineraries in one place.
￼
Business Problem
Travelers already want to book experiences together on Roamly, but lack of group planning, payment coordination, and scheduling tools creates friction, resulting in high drop-off rates and lost bookings.
￼
Target Segment
Friends planning vacations together
Why:
Largest and most active potential group segment
Already demonstrating demand through workaround behaviors
Naturally creates invitation-driven user growth
￼
Value Proposition
Roamly Groups helps friends plan, coordinate, and pay for travel experiences together without juggling texts, spreadsheets, or payment apps.
￼
GTM Motion
Hybrid GTM
Primary: Product-Led Growth (PLG) Users create a group trip, invite friends, split payments, and complete bookings directly in the product.
Secondary: Community-Led Growth (CLG) Every organizer brings additional travelers into Roamly through invitations, shared itineraries, and collaborative trip planning.
Why this motion
The value is realized immediately, requires no sales interaction, and becomes more valuable as additional friends join the trip.
￼
Primary Goal
Acquire
Use group planning as an acquisition engine.
Every trip organizer becomes a distribution channel that introduces new travelers to Roamly.
Secondary Goal
Expand booking volume among existing customers by reducing group booking friction.
￼
Channel Strategy
1. In-Product Growth Loops (Primary Channel)
Launch "Plan with Friends" entry points throughout booking flows
One-click friend invitations via link, SMS, WhatsApp, and email
Incentivize completing group setup before checkout
Why: Highest-intent users already exist on the platform.
2. Referral & Sharing Programs
Reward organizers when invited friends complete their first booking
Create shareable group itinerary pages
Why: Reinforces CLG and lowers acquisition costs.
3. Travel Community Partnerships
Promote Roamly Groups in travel communities, Reddit travel forums, Facebook travel groups, and creator channels
Why: The target audience already discovers travel experiences through communities.
4. Social Content
Showcase "Group Trip Made Easy" use cases on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube
Why: Vacation planning is highly social and visually driven.
￼
Success Metrics
North Star Metric
New travelers acquired through Roamly Groups invitations
This directly measures whether the acquisition-focused PLG + CLG motion is working.
Supporting Metrics
Number of groups created
Invitation acceptance rate
Group booking conversion rate
Average group size
Split-payment adoption rate
First booking rate of invited users
Cost per acquired traveler (CPA)
￼
Core Hypothesis
If Roamly enables travelers to seamlessly coordinate bookings, payments, and itineraries with friends, then existing users will invite additional travelers onto the platform, increasing both group booking conversion and customer acquisition through network-driven growth.
